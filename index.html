Legal advice. We don't tell you which option is best for you, or whether your application will be accepted. For those questions, we connect you with a partner lawyer, with your file already prepared.

Frequently asked questions

Are you lawyers?

No. We are people who went through these procedures ourselves. For any legal advice, we put you in touch with a lawyer.

Do you guarantee I will get the visa?

No, nobody can. The decision always belongs to the administration. We help you submit a complete and clear file.

What happens to my data?

The questionnaire asks only for the minimum. Documents are requested only once you agree, and you can ask for them to be deleted at any time.
